CAS R04 · Offline log packager

This laptop utility is not connected to the rover. It does not poll the main ESP32, receive live camera streams or send motor commands. Use it after the survey to bundle the phone's sensor log with original JPEGs downloaded from the camera microSD archive.

Maximum combined upload: 500 MB. Files are preserved as supplied and checksummed. This utility packages the data; it does not invent indoor coordinates or generate motor commands.

Analysis workflow

Download the ZIP and attach it to the analysis conversation. The log and original images can then be reviewed together. Any resulting route plan must remain a draft until its coordinate frame and localization are independently validated. A compact plan can be pasted into the phone dashboard's navigation-plan box; the current R04 firmware stores it but does not execute it.
